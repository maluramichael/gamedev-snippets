# snake-serpenoid

A chain of twelve links moved by one sine wave: every joint bends by `2 alpha sin(omega t + i beta) + gamma`, each one a little later than its neighbour (Hirose's serpenoid curve, equation 1 of Colorado et al. 2010). Nothing aims the body. The shape is all the formula gives, where the body goes is decided by the ground, which `logic.hpp` models as friction at the middle of every link, stronger across the link than along it. With the same friction everywhere ("Ice") the snake wiggles in place, with a strong side grip ("Wheels") the wave turns into forward motion of about 0.88 body lengths per cycle for the default setting. The sliders are the source's three parameters: `a` (bend), `b` (waves on the body) and `c` (steering bias, a circle instead of a straight line). Orange bars mark the sideways speed of each link, the thing the wheels forbid. The stride readout is measured over one wave period of simulated time. The logic is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
