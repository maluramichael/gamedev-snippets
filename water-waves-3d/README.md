# water-waves-3d

A 201x201 vertex grid that the GPU bends with six Gerstner waves (WebGL2 / OpenGL ES 3): horizontal and vertical displacement per wave, deep-water dispersion `omega = sqrt(g k)`, exact normals from the analytic derivatives, and foam where the Jacobian of the horizontal shift gets small. The fragment shader mixes Schlick-Fresnel sky reflection, deep teal, sun glint, foam and horizon fog. A buoy floats on the same water: the CPU mirrors the shader maths (`logic.hpp`) and inverts the horizontal shift with Newton's method to find the surface point under its mooring. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, the chips set the wind and switch wireframe, foam and pause. The wave maths is tested natively (`test.cpp`: dispersion, crest and trough heights, periodicity, normals against central differences, the steepness budget and what happens above it, the buoy), the rendering needs a browser.
