# storyteller-timelines

Three RimWorld storytellers on the same 200 days. Cassandra and Phoebe follow a calendar of "on" windows (Cassandra 4.6 days on and 6 off, one or two major threats at least 1.9 days apart; Phoebe 8 on and 8 off, always exactly one), Randy has none: a weighted coin every 1000 ticks, an event about every 1.35 days, about 15 percent of them a major threat, and a forced threat when 13 days passed without one (hollow marker). The timeline shows one colony, with the threat count per 60-day year. Next to it a histogram of the days between major attacks, pooled over 100 colonies of 1200 days: Randy's mean gap is almost the same as Cassandra's, the shape is not. Show one storyteller or all three, R rolls a new colony and replays it, P replays the same one, Space skips to day 200. The numbers are from the RimWorld wiki and were not checked against the game files; where inside a window a threat falls is this demo's choice (uniform). The rules are in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`, including measurements over long runs against the wiki's yearly rate and mean gap.
