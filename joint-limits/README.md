# joint-limits

An arm of four segments follows the mouse, and every joint has an allowed range, drawn as a wedge on its parent segment. The arm is solved with CCD (cyclic coordinate descent) in angle space, so the segment lengths never change. Three ways to deal with the ranges can be switched at any time: no limits, solve without limits and clamp the finished pose (the faint orange chain is the unclamped solution, the tip misses by the gap), or clamp every joint inside every solver step. The workspace map asks the same question for a grid of targets, solved from the rest pose: teal where the tip lands on the target, orange where it misses. With the arm limits it counts 719 reached cells without limits, 416 with a clamp in every step and 102 with a clamp at the end (of 778 cells inside the reach). The edges of the wedges can be dragged, the presets switch between an arm-like range set and a stiff one. CCD with clamping can get stuck in a pose that is not the best one (a straight arm with a pinned wrist never folds), the panel says so, R restarts the pose. The logic is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
