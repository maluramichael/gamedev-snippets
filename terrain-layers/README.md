# terrain-layers

Terrain from three layers of noise and four ways to blend them. The continent layer (slow fBm) decides land or sea, the mountain layer is ridged fBm (crests where the noise crosses its middle), the detail layer is fast fBm. Plain adding gives every place the same relief, so mountain ranges stand in the sea. Multiplying makes the relief grow with the continent value. The mask (smoothstep over the continent value, threshold and softness on sliders) lets the mountains in only where the land is high, and lerp fades between a flat plains terrain and the full one. The cards switch a layer off, the chips switch blend mode and view (terrain, height, relief as slope), N rolls a new seed, O tints the area where the mask is off, dragging the map or the arrow keys pan it. Below the map the demo prints the mean slope on the lowlands against the highlands (mask clearly off against clearly on) and their ratio, so "same relief everywhere" is a number: about 1 for add, about 2.5 for the mask. The noise is written from scratch (hash, Perlin gradients, fade curve, fBm), all of it in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp` (hand-computed blend modes, mask edges, fBm normalization, relief statistics, hillshade, pan shift).
