# wireworld-gates

Wireworld with four cell states (empty, wire, electron head, electron tail) and one counting rule: a wire becomes a head when exactly one or two of its eight neighbours are heads. That is enough for clocks, diodes and logic gates. The field is a circuit you can paint on, and the presets (clock, diode, OR, XOR, half adder) are plain cells. For the gates the demo plays the test bench: at the start of every round it injects an electron into each switched-on input, and the truth table next to the field is measured by running every input combination on a copy of the current circuit, so a circuit you edit gets a new table. The two rules marked bug are the usual implementation mistakes (a wire fires on any head neighbour, or the grid is updated in place) and show what they do to the gates. The cell rule, the circuits and the measurement are in `logic.hpp` and `circuits.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
