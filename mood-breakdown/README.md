# mood-breakdown

Eight colonists with a mood bar each. The bar chases a target (the dark triangle) at most +12 per hour up and -8 per hour down, and the target is a base value plus a personal offset plus the thoughts that are currently active. Below three lines (35, 20 and 5 for a plain colonist, other values for the steady and the nervous one) a break becomes possible, at a constant rate with a mean time of 4, 0.8 and 0.5 days. Fire events for everybody or for the selected colonist (corpse, bad meal, funeral), drag the base mood slider, pick a speed. A break scares the two neighbours, which is how one failure becomes several (orange dot, "chained" in the count). The second model, switched with the Dwarf Fortress chip, adds a slow long-term counter: only when it is high as well does anybody break, so a bad day alone stays harmless for a while. The numbers for the bar speed, the lines and the mean times are from the RimWorld wiki and were not checked against the game files, the thought values, the break duration and the chain reaction are made up for the demo. The rules are in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`, including measurements over many seeds.
