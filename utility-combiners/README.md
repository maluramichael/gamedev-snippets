# utility-combiners

Utility AI for one villager. Four actions (eat, sleep, work, wander) each score a few considerations between 0 and 1: a need such as hunger run through an editable response curve (linear, quadratic or logistic), plus a fact about the world such as the food in the larder or the daylight. A combiner folds them into one score per action and the highest score wins. The mean lets one zero factor hide among good ones, the product gives every factor a veto, bands rank the survival actions above any score of an ordinary one. The panel on the right shows what each combiner would pick in the current state, the two scenario chips jump to states where they disagree, and dragging a bar sets a need by hand. The timeline shows the scores of the last 60 seconds, the strip below it the chosen action: it flickers whenever two scores keep crossing, and a bonus for the running action (inertia) calms that down. The scoring is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
