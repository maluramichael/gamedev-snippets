# redundancy-nullspace

A planar arm with four segments holds its hand on a target. The hand has two coordinates, the arm four joint angles, so a two dimensional family of poses puts the hand in the same place. Each fixed step (60 per second) the joints move by `J+ e + (I - J+ J) phi`: the pseudoinverse term brings the hand to the target, the projector `I - J+ J` cuts a free wish `phi` down to the part that leaves the hand where it is. The chips pick the wish: off, back to a rest pose, away from the joint limits (only the outer half of each range pushes), or relaxed (gradient of the mean absolute joint angle). Dragging an elbow or shaking (`S`) moves the arm inside the family while the hand stays on the target. `P` switches the projection off, the wish then acts on the hand too and the error shows. The panel shows the hand error, the self motion (the part of the step inside the null space), the mean angle, a history and where each joint sits in its range. Dragging elsewhere in the field places the target, `A` lets it wander, `R` resets. The joint limits are only shown, not enforced. The logic is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
