# bid-rent-town

Town rings from bid rents. Market, house and farm each have a straight bid curve over the distance to the town centre, and every cell takes the use with the highest bid. Nobody draws rings, they fall out of the maximum. The graph on the right shows the three curves for the cell under the cursor, the heavy line is the maximum and its colour is the winner, dotted lines mark where it changes. Sliders set the town size (it scales the market and house curves), the pull of the river (a bonus for market and house near the water) and the slope of each curve. Clicking or dragging on the map moves the centre, G grows and shrinks the town. "Hand-set rings" is the naive version with radii typed in once: it follows the size but ignores slopes and river. The curve shapes and all numbers are made up for the demo. The logic is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp` (ring order, tie at the border, growth, river bank, a use that never wins).
