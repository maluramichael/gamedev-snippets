# jacobian-singularity

A planar arm with three segments chases a target that wanders along the edge of its reach. Each fixed step (60 per second) one iteration of the chosen method turns the error into joint angle steps through the Jacobian: the transpose (`d = alpha J^T e`), the pseudoinverse (`d = J^T (J J^T)^-1 e`) or damped least squares (`d = J^T (J J^T + lambda^2 I)^-1 e`). Near the stretched pose the Jacobian loses a direction, its smallest singular value goes to 0 and the pseudoinverse answers a pixel of error with a swing of whole radians, so the arm jitters. Damping caps the swing at `|e| / (2 lambda)`, the clamp (`C`) shortens the error to about half a segment. The panel shows the smallest singular value, the joint step and the error, plus a history of the step sizes. Dragging in the field places the target by hand, `A` lets it wander again, `[` and `]` change lambda, `R` resets. The orange bar at the hand is the direction it can barely move in. The logic is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
