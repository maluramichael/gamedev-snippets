# fabrik-vs-ccd

One chain of ten segments follows the mouse, solved three ways from the pose it already has, a few iterations every 1/60 s as a game would do it. CCD turns joint by joint from the tip to the root. FABRIK puts the tip on the target and slides the points along lines, backward to the root and forward again, so every segment keeps its length by construction. Relaxation pins both ends and lets every segment share its length error. The faint poses are the steps inside the last iteration (P and N pause and step by hand), the panel shows the tip error, the total bend, the length error and how many iterations each solver needs from the start pose to reach the current target. Beyond the reach FABRIK stretches the chain straight in one iteration, CCD creeps there and relaxation can only stretch its segments. The Stuck chip sets up a straight two-segment chain with the target on the same line, where all three freeze until the target leaves the line. The math is in `logic.hpp` and covered by `test.cpp`.
